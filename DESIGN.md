# Material workshop design

The worksheet is the visual authority. Use native Material 3 components and their type scale, a teal seed (`0xFF0F766E`), a centered AppBar and restrained surfaces. The course Card retains the required radius and margin of 16. Final colors resolve through ColorScheme and text through TextTheme. Preserve the profile order: portrait, identity, contact card, statistics, equal-width adjacent buttons. Content scrolls vertically on small windows and is width-limited on desktop. No decorative motion or added product features.

An alternate purple seed demonstrates the theme exercise. Both brightness schemes derive from the same seed. Screenshots are labeled as Flutter Web captures.
